WordPress vs Shopify for Small Businesses in India

Both platforms power successful online stores in India. The right choice depends on your budget, how much control you want and who will manage the website day to day. This guide compares them on the points that matter most to a small business.

By Himanshu Bisoyi, freelance website developer · November 2026

WordPress here means a self-hosted WordPress website with the free WooCommerce plugin for selling online.

When WordPress is the better choice

  • You want full ownership of the website, data and design.
  • You plan to publish articles, guides or news to attract visitors from Google.
  • You need custom features, such as bookings, donations, quotes or member areas.
  • You prefer a lower running cost over three to five years.

When Shopify is the better choice

  • You sell products only and want to launch quickly.
  • You do not want to think about hosting, updates or security.
  • Your team is non-technical and will manage orders every day.
  • You are comfortable with a monthly subscription and paid apps as you grow.

Costs to plan for

Prices change, so confirm current plans before you decide. In general:

  • WordPress + WooCommerce: domain (about ₹1,000–1,200 per year), hosting (about ₹3,000–10,000 per year), optional premium theme or plugins, and maintenance.
  • Shopify: monthly plan, paid apps for extra features, and possibly a Shopify transaction fee when you use a third-party gateway such as Razorpay.
  • Both: payment gateway charges of around 2% per transaction, and the one-time cost of design and setup.

My recommendation

For most small Indian businesses that sell a modest number of products and also want to be found on Google, WordPress with WooCommerce gives the best value and flexibility. If you sell many products, want the simplest daily management and prefer not to deal with hosting, Shopify is an excellent choice. Frequently asked questions

Is WordPress or Shopify cheaper in India?

Over three years, WordPress with WooCommerce is usually cheaper for a small store, because the software is free and you only pay for hosting, the domain and any premium plugins. Shopify has a monthly subscription, and many features come from paid apps.

Can I move from Shopify to WordPress later, or the other way round?

Yes. Products, customers and orders can be exported and imported, but the design must be rebuilt and old links redirected, so it is easier to choose carefully at the start.

Which is better for SEO?

Both can rank well on Google. WordPress gives more control over URLs, page structure and content, which suits businesses that plan to publish articles regularly. Shopify covers the basics well for product stores.

Which payment gateways work in India?

Both work with Indian gateways such as Razorpay, PayU and Cashfree. On Shopify, check the extra transaction fee that applies when you use a third-party gateway on your plan.
