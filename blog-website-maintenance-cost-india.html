Website Maintenance Cost in India (2026)

A website is not finished on launch day. Software updates, backups, security and small changes keep it fast, safe and bringing in enquiries. This guide explains what maintenance includes, what it typically costs in India and how to decide what you need.

By Himanshu Bisoyi, freelance website developer · November 2026

Indicative ranges for 2026. The right plan depends on how often your website changes.

What maintenance includes

  • Updates: WordPress core, theme and plugin updates, tested so nothing breaks.
  • Backups: regular, off-site backups so the website can be restored quickly.
  • Security: monitoring, firewall and malware checks, and fixing issues early.
  • Speed and uptime: keeping pages fast and checking the site stays online.
  • Small changes: text, images, prices and contact details updated on request.
  • Technical fixes: broken forms, errors and compatibility issues.

Costs that are separate

These are normal yearly costs, but they are not part of maintenance:

  • Domain renewal: about ₹1,000–1,200 per year for a .com.
  • Hosting renewal: about ₹3,000–10,000 per year.
  • Premium plugins or Shopify apps: depending on the features you use.
  • New work: new pages, redesigns and new features are quoted separately.

What happens without maintenance

  • The website slowly becomes slower and less secure as software goes out of date.
  • A plugin update can break the layout or the contact form without anyone noticing.
  • Hacked websites can be flagged by Google, which hurts trust and rankings.
  • Fixing a neglected website often costs more than a year of maintenance.

Frequently asked questions

How much does website maintenance cost in India?

Most small business websites spend about ₹5,000–₹60,000 a year on maintenance, depending on how often the site changes and whether it has an online store. My maintenance plan is ₹24,000 per year after the free period.

Is website maintenance really necessary?

Yes, for any website that brings enquiries or sales. Without updates and backups, WordPress websites become slower, can break after plugin updates and are more likely to be hacked.

What is not included in maintenance?

New pages, redesigns, new features and large content uploads are usually quoted separately. Domain and hosting renewals are paid directly to the provider.

Can I maintain the website myself?

You can update text and images yourself on WordPress or Shopify. Technical tasks such as updates, backups, security and fixing errors are best handled by a developer.
