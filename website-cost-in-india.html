Website Development Cost in India (2026)

In 2026, a small business website in India typically costs ₹15,000–₹1,00,000, a company website ₹40,000–₹1,50,000 and an online store ₹40,000–₹3,00,000. This guide explains what drives those prices, what you should expect to pay each year after launch, and how to budget for the right website.

What affects the price

Two websites that look similar can be priced very differently. These factors make the biggest difference:

  • Number of pages. Each page requires design, content placement and testing.
  • Custom design or template. A design created for your brand costs more than an adapted template, but looks distinctive.
  • E-commerce functionality. Cart, checkout, customer accounts and order notifications add significant development work.
  • Content. Providing ready text and photographs reduces cost; copywriting and product uploads are charged separately.
  • Integrations. Payment gateways, CRMs, booking systems and delivery partners each add setup and testing time.
  • Timeline. Urgent delivery requires additional hours and may increase the price.

Freelancer, agency or website builder?

Each option suits a different type of project.

Website builder

The lowest upfront cost, but you invest your own time, designs tend to look generic and you are limited by the platform.

Suited to: testing an idea

Freelancer

Direct access to the developer at a lower cost than an agency. Choose someone experienced who offers maintenance after launch.

Suited to: most small and medium businesses

Agency

A team of designers, developers and project managers. Higher cost, appropriate for large projects with many stakeholders.

Suited to: large or complex projects

How to control your budget

  • Launch with the pages you need now and add further sections later.
  • Prepare your text, logo and photographs before the project begins.
  • Choose WordPress if your team will update content regularly.
  • Budget for maintenance rather than rebuilding the website every few years.
  • Ensure the domain and hosting are registered to you and that you receive full admin access.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a basic website cost in India?

A basic business website of five to ten pages typically costs ₹15,000–₹1,00,000 in 2026, depending on whether it is built by a freelancer or an agency and how much custom design it needs. My business website package starts at ₹35,000.

Are very cheap websites worth it?

Rarely, for a business. Very low-cost websites are often unmodified templates with no ongoing support and slow hosting, and the domain is sometimes registered in the seller's name. Make sure the domain is registered to you and that maintenance is available.

Are there yearly costs after launch?

Yes. The domain (around ₹1,000–1,200 per year for a .com) and hosting (₹3,000–10,000 per year) must be renewed annually. Maintenance is optional but recommended to keep the website secure and up to date.

How long does it take to build a website?

Approximately one week for a landing page, two to three weeks for a business website, four to six weeks for a company website and around two months for an e-commerce store.

Can the payment be made in instalments?

Yes. My standard terms are 50% in advance and 50% before the website goes live.
