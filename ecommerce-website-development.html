WooCommerce or Shopify?

I develop on both platforms and recommend the one that best fits your budget and operations.

WooCommerce (WordPress)

  • One-time development cost with no platform subscription
  • Full control over design, features and customer data
  • Hosting typically costs ₹6,000–10,000 per year
  • Well suited to most Indian D2C brands and manufacturers

Shopify

  • Hosted platform that is simple to manage
  • Monthly Shopify subscription plus app fees
  • Fast to launch and well suited to international selling
  • Less flexible for highly custom requirements

How orders are processed

Once the store is live, most of the order flow runs automatically.

  1. Add to cartThe customer selects products and adds them to the cart.
  2. Secure paymentThe customer enters a delivery address and pays by UPI, card or net banking.
  3. ConfirmationA confirmation page is shown and an "Order confirmed" email is sent.
  4. DispatchYour team packs the order and adds the courier tracking ID in the admin panel.
  5. Shipping updateThe customer automatically receives a "Shipped" email with tracking details.

No. Order processing, packing, shipping and refunds are handled by your team. The store automatically sends "Order confirmed" and "Shipped" emails, including the courier tracking ID.
