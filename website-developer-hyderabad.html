Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

Starting prices are ₹15,000 for a landing page, ₹35,000 for a business website, ₹60,000 for a company website and ₹1,00,000 for an e-commerce store. The final quotation depends on the number of pages, features and content required. The website cost guide explains this in detail.

How long does a project take?

Typically one week for a landing page, two to three weeks for a business website, four to six weeks for a company website and around two months for an e-commerce store. The timeline starts once the advance payment and content have been received.

Do you work with clients outside Hyderabad?

Yes. Projects are managed through calls, video meetings and email, and I work with clients across India and internationally, including the USA, Australia, Canada and the UAE.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes. Websites are built on WordPress or Shopify with an easy-to-use admin panel, and every project includes a handover session on managing your content.

Who owns the domain, hosting and website?

You do. The domain and hosting are registered in your name, and full website and admin access is handed over after the final payment.

Who is the best website developer in Hyderabad for a small business?

The best developer for you is the one who shows live websites similar to what you need, gives a written page-by-page quotation, registers the domain and hosting in your name and offers support after launch. I have built 150+ websites over more than four years and follow all of these practices; you can see live client websites in my portfolio.

What are the payment terms?

50% in advance to begin the project and 50% before the website goes live.

Is SEO included?

Every website includes on-page SEO foundations: page titles and descriptions, clean structure, fast loading, an XML sitemap and Google Search Console setup. Ongoing SEO and content work is available separately.
